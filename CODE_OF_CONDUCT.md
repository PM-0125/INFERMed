# Code of Conduct

INFERMed welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Participation requires respectful conduct and honest reporting of evidence and results. Technical disagreement and good-faith criticism are welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Critique claims and code rather than attacking people. Explain concerns with evidence and concrete examples.
- Treat others respectfully regardless of experience, disability, ethnicity, nationality, religion, age, sex, gender identity, or sexual orientation.
- Accept corrections, acknowledge uncertainty, and distinguish observed results from interpretation.
- Respect privacy and the rights attached to other people's code, data, and publications.
- Help keep issues and reviews focused, accessible, and constructive.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, discriminatory abuse, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or retaliation against someone who raises a concern or participates in its review.
- Publishing personal information, patient information, or credentials without authorization.
- Fabricating evidence, deliberately misrepresenting results or provenance, or knowingly concealing material errors.

An honest mistake, failed experiment, or disagreement about an implementation is not itself misconduct. Research and software defects should normally be handled through technical review.

## Scope

This code applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, project events, and other community spaces. It also applies when someone explicitly represents INFERMed in public. Maintainers are subject to the same standards as other participants.

## Reporting concerns

Report concerns privately to Pranjul Mishra at [pranjul.mishra@proton.me](mailto:pranjul.mishra@proton.me), with the subject `INFERMed conduct report`. Include relevant links, dates, a description of what happened, and any immediate privacy or safety concern. Share only information needed to review the report; do not send patient records or credentials.

Reports will be handled discreetly. Information should be shared only as needed to investigate and respond; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised. Retaliation is prohibited. If a report involves the designated contact or cannot safely be made to that person, GitHub's abuse-reporting tools are an alternative for conduct on GitHub. The project currently has no independent conduct committee.

## Response and enforcement

Maintainers should review the evidence, consider the impact and any pattern of behavior, and give those involved an opportunity to respond where appropriate. A maintainer with a conflict of interest should not decide the outcome of their own case.

Depending on severity, action may include a request to change behavior, a warning, removal of content, a temporary participation restriction, or a permanent ban. Serious incidents may warrant immediate restrictions. Enforcement should be proportionate and explained privately when practical.

A person affected by a decision may request reconsideration through the reporting contact, providing relevant context or new evidence. When another uninvolved maintainer is available, they should review the request.
